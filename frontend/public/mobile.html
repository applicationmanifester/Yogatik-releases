How to install

Step-by-step guide for both platforms.

1

Download the APK

Tap Download APK above. Your browser will save the .apk file to Downloads.

2

Allow install from unknown sources

Go to Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps and enable your browser or Files app.

3

Open the APK and install

Tap the downloaded file in your notifications or Files app, then tap Install. The app icon will appear on your home screen.

4

Sign in (optional)

Open Yogatik and sign in with Google to sync your conversations — or use it entirely offline with a local Ollama model.

1

Install AltStore on your iPhone

Download AltStore from altstore.io using the AltServer desktop companion (Mac or Windows).

2

Download the IPA

Tap Download IPA above and save the .ipa file. Transfer it to your iPhone via AirDrop, Files, or iCloud Drive.

3

Install via AltStore

In AltStore on your iPhone, tap My Apps → + and select the Yogatik IPA. AltStore will sign and install it with your Apple ID (free account works).

4

Trust the developer certificate

Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, find your Apple ID under Developer App, and tap Trust.
