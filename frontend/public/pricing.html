Frequently Asked Questions

How does the desktop license work?

When you subscribe to Pro on the web, your entitlement is signed cryptographically and linked to your account. When you log into the Yogatik Studio Desktop app, all native tools (terminal, file system, browser automation) unlock automatically.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time via your UPI app or billing portal. You will retain access until the end of your current prepaid period.
