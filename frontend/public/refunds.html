Cancellation & Refund Policy

Clear, transparent billing terms for Yogatik Pro subscriptions and digital services.

Last updated 1 September 2026.

1. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your Yogatik Pro subscription at any time without penalty or cancellation fees.

  • How to cancel: You can manage or cancel your recurring subscription directly from your payment provider's dashboard, UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM e-mandates), or by emailing our support team at applicationmanifester@gmail.com.
  • Access after cancellation: If you cancel your subscription, your Pro benefits remain active until the end of your current paid billing period (monthly or yearly). No further charges will be made once cancelled.

2. Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with Yogatik. Because Yogatik is a digital software product with an immediate license delivery upon purchase:

  • Initial Purchase Refund (7-day window): If you are unsatisfied with your subscription or purchased by mistake, you can request a full refund within 7 days of your initial purchase date by emailing applicationmanifester@gmail.com with your payment transaction ID.
  • Duplicate or Erroneous Charges: If you were charged more than once for the same billing cycle due to a technical error, we will issue an immediate 100% refund for the duplicate transaction.
  • Processing Time: Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method (Bank Account / UPI / Card) within 5 to 7 business days as per standard banking processing schedules.

3. Digital Service Delivery

Yogatik Pro is a digital license entitlement. Upon successful payment verification by Razorpay or Paddle, your Pro status and capabilities are unlocked automatically within seconds in both the Web application and Yogatik Studio Desktop app.

If you have any questions about billing, cancellations, or refund requests, please contact us directly:
