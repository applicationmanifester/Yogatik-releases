Cancellation & Refund Policy

Clear billing and cancellation terms for Yogatik Pro subscriptions and digital licenses.

Last updated 1 September 2026.

1. No Refund Policy (All Sales Are Final)

Yogatik Pro is an intangible, digital software product with immediate digital license delivery and compute activation upon purchase.

All purchases and subscription payments are strictly final and non-refundable. We do not offer refunds, prorated credits, or charge reversals for any completed billing period once digital access has been unlocked.

Before subscribing, users are encouraged to explore Yogatik's free features and trial capabilities to ensure it meets their requirements.

2. Subscription Cancellation & Auto-Renewal

You may cancel your recurring subscription at any time to prevent future automated charges:

  • How to cancel: You can pause or cancel your subscription directly from your payment app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM e-mandate section), through the billing portal link in your email receipt, or by contacting applicationmanifester@gmail.com.
  • Timing: To avoid being charged for the next billing cycle, cancellation must be completed prior to your scheduled renewal date.
  • Access after cancellation: When you cancel, your Pro features remain fully accessible until the conclusion of your current paid billing period (monthly or yearly). No subsequent renewals will be charged.

3. Immediate Digital Delivery

Upon successful payment authorization via Razorpay or Paddle, your Yogatik Pro entitlement is activated automatically within seconds on both the Web application and Yogatik Studio Desktop app on your signed-in account.

If you have any questions regarding your billing status or need assistance with your subscription, please reach out to:
